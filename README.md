# ACME Frontier AI

> **Failing Forward.**

The advanced research division of ACME Corporation.

ACME Frontier AI is a working laboratory for human–AI collaboration. We study how people and agents build software together, where that collaboration breaks down, and what can be recovered from the wreckage. Most of what we ship does not work as intended. This is by design — and, increasingly, on purpose.

## Heritage

ACME Frontier AI continues a long institutional tradition of ambitious engineering and instructive outcomes. Where earlier divisions specialized in delivery systems, kinetic solutions, and applied gravity, this division applies the same methodology to language models: build the contraption, deploy it with confidence, observe the result, document why it detonated, and revise.

The Road Runner remains uncaught. Research continues.

## What this is

A public testbed. A place to run experiments in:

- **Skills** — capabilities and disciplines for agents
- **Rules** — encoded judgment and guardrails
- **Agents** — specialized operators for specific work
- **Workflows** — repeatable procedures for getting things done
- **Tools** — instruments for measuring and steering the work
- **Philosophy** — sustainable ways of working with AI that keep the human in the loop and out of pure extraction mode

Experiments ship early, often, and without apology. The ones that survive contact with reality graduate to their own facilities (repositories). The rest stay here as documented findings — including, especially, the failures.

## How we work

- **We ship experiments.** Perfection is the enemy of evidence. An idea in the open beats a better idea in your head.
- **Failures are the product.** Every backfire is logged — what it was for, how it broke, and why. The paper trail is the point.
- **We measure; we don't trust.** Self-report is not evidence. *Done* means it was seen to run.
- **We document the *why*, not just the *what*.** A reason reconstructed after the fact is not a receipt. Where the reason wasn't captured, the record says so.
- **The work is public.** Take what's useful. Borrow freely. Attribution appreciated, not required.

## Research areas

- Decoding the model black box — what's actually happening inside the collaboration
- Sustainable human–AI partnership — keeping your humanity while working with agents every day
- Behavioral conditioning of agents — shaping *how* they work, not just *what* they produce
- Drift, degradation, and knowing when to pull the plug
